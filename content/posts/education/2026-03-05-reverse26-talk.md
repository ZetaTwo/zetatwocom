In March, I gave a presentation at [RE//verse 2026](https://re-verse.io) titled "Reversing Age of Empires 2: Upgrading tools from Dark Age to Castle Age".
I talked about how reverse engineering Age of Empires 2 (Definitive Edition) led me to develop automation and plugins for Binary Ninja to decrypt and de-obfuscate its code, including a query system for pattern matching BNIL instruction sequences.
The [recorded talk is available on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dp-IvqfCoL0) and you can [download the slides here](/assets/other/reverse26-aoe2-slides.pdf).
